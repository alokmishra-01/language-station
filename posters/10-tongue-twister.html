an n with the tongue curled back — English has no such letter

Curl the tongue back and you get the retroflex sounds — the ones that make an Indian accent sound Indian. Every Indian language has them; almost no European language does. Tap ତ and ଟ on the screen one after the other and listen for the difference. Most people can't hear it the first time and can't stop hearing it after the third.
