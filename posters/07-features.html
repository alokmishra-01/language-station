Talk to
Smruti.

Put the headset on and have a real conversation with an Odia speaker. She'll teach you words, then ask you to say them back.

Try saying

"How do you say hello?"

  • "What's your name?"
  • "Teach me to count."
  • "How do I say thank you?"
  • "Tell me something about Odisha."
  • Then say the Odia word back — and watch her face light up.

You ask in English. She answers in Odia and explains. She can't grade your pronunciation — she'll just cheer you on. Three and a half minutes each, then the screen resets for the next person.
