Why is Odia
round?

Because for centuries it was not written on paper. It was scratched into a dried palm leaf with an iron stylus — and a palm leaf has a grain, like wood.

A straight line runs with the grain and slices the leaf clean in two. Your page falls apart in your hands.

A curve keeps crossing the grain and never follows it far enough to tear. So the letters curved — and stayed curved.
