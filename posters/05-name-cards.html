The thing they walk away holding. Four A6 cards per sheet. Print 15–25 sheets (= 60–100 cards), guillotine or scissor down the dashed lines before the event.
How it works at the table: visitor types their name on the screen, sees it in Odia — then copies it by hand into the cream box. Copying an unfamiliar script is the moment it stops being decoration and starts being writing. Put out fine-tip black pens.
