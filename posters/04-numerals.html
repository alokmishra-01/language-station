That circle is a big deal. Treating zero as a number you can actually calculate with — add it, subtract it, and state the rules — was worked out in India. Brahmagupta set those rules down in 628 CE, centuries before the idea reached Europe. Every empty box you have ever left blank on a maths paper owes it something.
