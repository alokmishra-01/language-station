Three standing signs, no stands needed. Print 1 set, fold each sheet in half along the dashed line and stand it up like a tent — the lower half is printed upside down on purpose, so the sign reads from both sides of the table.
These do the wayfinding on a small table: one sign per zone, so you are not repeating yourself out loud for four hours.
