Odia Language Station · what you can do

two minutes each.

Everything runs on this Mac. Pick any one — you don't have to start at the top.

Talk to Smruti

Put the headset on and have a real conversation. She'll teach you words to say back.

  • Try: "How do you say hello?" · "Teach me to count." · "Tell me something about Odisha."
  • Then say the Odia word back to her.

Your name in Odia

Type your name and watch it turn into Odia writing — then hear it read back.

  • Take a card from the table and copy your name onto it by hand before you go.

One honest thing

Ask Smruti anything in English — she will answer in Odia and explain. That is not a gimmick: the speech system behind her only understands English ears, so English in, Odia out. She also can't mark your pronunciation. She will cheer you on regardless, which is roughly what a grandmother would do anyway.
