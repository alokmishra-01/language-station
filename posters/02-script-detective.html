Your clue

Odia was written with an iron stylus on a dried palm leaf. A straight line cut along the grain would slit the leaf in two — so Odia letters learned to curve. Look for the roundest one. Look for the umbrella on top.

Which one is Odia? Write the letter, then come and check at the screen.
